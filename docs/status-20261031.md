### Moments: a screen with no way out, a caption drawn twice, and a picture that never filled the screen

Moments and its full-screen viewer had never been rendered. Rendered, in order:
`evidence/mom-before-0.png` (the tab), `evidence/mview-before-0.png` (a photo
Moment), and the clip tile.

**There was no way out.** Moments is a pushed screen with `headerShown: false`,
and it drew a giant *Moments* title with no back control — while Search,
Circles, Chat and Settings all draw one. Arriving from the Moments row on
Discover, the only exits were the swipe gesture and Android's back button.

**The caption was drawn twice, and the larger copy landed on the reply box.**
`MomentPreview` draws the words *inside* the picture, and the viewer then drew
them again as its own band underneath. On a photo Moment the same sentence
appeared at 26px inside the photo and at 17px below it, and the two collided
with the reply box at the bottom of an 844pt screen.

**The picture never filled the screen.** The viewer handed the preview
`padding: space.xl`, and the preview applied that same style to the media as
well, so a portrait Moment was drawn as a **342 × 796 photo inside a 390 × 844
screen** — black columns down both sides and a bright strip where the inset
ended. Measured in the DOM, not guessed.

**The reply box was an outline only.** `border: 1px rgba(255,255,255,0.35)` on
whatever the photo happened to be, so "Reply to Ngozi…" sat as grey text on
magenta and the field was invisible.

**Every tile collided with itself.** The author's face was absolutely centred at
the bottom of the pebble — exactly where the Moment's words are — so "Six
minutes of waiting" was struck through by an avatar. Two identity cues on one
thumbnail, and the words lost.

What this commit changes:

- **A back button in the Moments header**, the same control Circles and Search
  use, so the screen can be left the way it was entered.
- **`MomentPreview` takes `caption`.** The viewer draws the one caption, in a
  rounded card with margins, over its own scrim.
- **Full-bleed media.** The viewer stops padding the picture; the preview's
  frame and its contents are now separate things, so the photo fills the screen.
- **Scrims top and bottom** (`transparent → rgba(0,0,0,0.88)`), so white type
  holds on a bright photo as well as a dark one, and **the reply box is a filled
  field** rather than an outline.
- **Tiles, rebuilt around the picture.** No face over the photo — the face moved
  next to the name underneath, where identity belongs; photo tiles carry no
  words; a **text** Moment still shows its words, since the words *are* the
  Moment; and a **clip** tile is a card with a play badge and its caption rather
  than a door for a paused decoder. Twelve video elements in a grid was also
  twelve decoders; now it is none.

Two things found along the way that are deliberately **not** in this commit:
a Moment has no stored cover, so a clip has nothing to show as a still — the
real fix is a poster captured when the Moment is posted, which belongs with the
upload work; and the viewer letterboxes a 9:16 clip on a 0.46 screen instead of
cover-cropping it, which needs a `fill` mode that crops rather than contains.

The harness grew `moments`, `moment`, `momentcomposer` and two clip variants
(`momentsvideo`, `momentvideo`) plus a words-only and a clip fixture, so the
second grid section ("Around you"), the text pebble and the playing branch are
all reachable. Evidence: `evidence/mom-after3-0.png`, `evidence/mview-after3-0.png`,
`evidence/mvidtab-after2-0.png`.

### The harness was lying, and that is why these two screens looked fine (`7625a9d`)

`Messages` crashed to a white page in the harness and rendered perfectly in
production — the mock, not the screen. It seeded `['conversations']` with the
*unwrapped* RPC reply (`{ items: [...] }`), string previews, `lastAt` and
`unreadCount`, none of which `list_conversations` or `ConversationSummary` ever
produce. Every field the row reads was either missing or a different type.

Three fixes, all in `devtools/`:

* **The cache seeds what the service returns**, not what the RPC returns.
* **The RPCs are answered at the transport** — `supabase.rpc` is swapped for a
  table in `mocks.ts`, in the `{ data, error }` envelope `service.rpc()`
  destructures. Screens that go through their real hooks (`useChat` calls the
  service directly, not the cache) now render for the first time.
* **The harness mounts the real `ErrorBoundary`**, so a harness crash looks like
  what a member sees rather than a white page.

`Messages` and `Chat` both draw; the fixture carries a run of three, a photo, a
day boundary and a read receipt so the grouping has something to get wrong.

### Video: the frame follows the picture, and it can be paused (`ed782c1`)

Two complaints, both real, both structural rather than taste.

**"The video is zoomed or cropped."** Three faults stacked:

1. The feed drew *every* post in a photo's frame — `mediaRatio ?? 0.8`, a 4:5
   box — because a video had no measured ratio of its own. A 16:9 clip in a 4:5
   box must be either cropped or letterboxed; there was no third option.
2. `position:absolute; inset:0` **does not size a replaced element**. The
   `<video>` stayed at its intrinsic 1280×720 inside a 348×435 box and was
   clipped from the top-left, while `object-fit` had no box to act in and did
   nothing. Explicit `width/height: 100%` is what makes `object-fit` real.
3. A `maxHeight` cap then squashed the frame's shape instead of narrowing it.

The rule now, in one line: **the frame follows the source unless the creator
chose a shape, and cropping happens only because of that choice.**
`lib/videoFrame.ts` decides it (`videoRatio`, `aspectRatioOf`, `videoFrame`,
`clampRatio` 0.3–3, `frameHeight`), and `EditedClip` measures the clip itself —
`videoWidth/videoHeight` from a metadata `<video>` on the web,
`player.videoTrack.size` polled every 250 ms on a phone.

**Measured in the render harness, not reasoned about:** a 1280×720 clip gets a
**348×196** frame (ratio 1.778, exact 16:9); a 720×1280 clip gets **293×520**,
keeping 9:16 by narrowing to the height cap instead of being squashed.

**"No pause and play animation."** `nativeControls` is deliberately off app-wide
(grey, covers captions, unstylable), so `PlayPauseFlash` replaces it: a
tap-to-toggle disc that springs in and fades while playing, stays while paused,
respects Reduce Motion, and reads its state from `player.playing` so an auto-stop
can never leave a lying "playing" disc. Proven: tapping flips the web `<video>`'s
`!paused` to false and `currentTime` holds.

Before/after: `evidence/video-before-after.png` (three panels),
`evidence/video-portrait-frame.png`.

`tsc --noEmit` clean; **157 library tests pass** (was 150), including 7 new cases
for the frame maths — null, zero, negative and NaN sizes degrade to the fallback
rather than to an Infinity/NaN style, which is a crash on Android.
